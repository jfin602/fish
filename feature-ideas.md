# Feature ideas

This is a parking place for optional ideas. It is not an execution queue or approval record. The [decision register](docs/decisions-and-questions.md) owns status, and [the roadmap](docs/roadmap.md) owns selected work.

## Ideas from the initial discussion

| Idea | Potential value | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Player-given fish names | Make individual lives easier to remember in history. | Proposed; not required for initial swimming. |
| Final-size portraits or silhouettes | Visually distinguish archived lives. | Proposed presentation detail. |
| Species/discovery journal across lives | Retain learned knowledge and support revisiting past discoveries. | Proposed; carryover rules unresolved. |
| Cosmetic unlocks | Provide a lasting reward while preserving a vulnerable hatchling start. | Proposed; no unlock economy selected. |
| Narrated recountings | Let players hear a fish's story. | Optional future presentation; text story remains sufficient. |
| Timeline or selected location imagery | Help illustrate actual life events. | Optional; no replay-recording system selected. |
| History filters and sorting | Keep a large collection easy to browse. | Candidate extension after basic archive access. |

## Keep confirmed requirements out of the backlog

Third-person 3D play, optional first person, hatchling-to-large growth, multi-session lives, permanent death, factual death recountings, and the main-menu history archive are confirmed scope, not optional ideas. Their owners are linked from [the documentation index](docs/README.md).

Add a new idea with its intended player value and unresolved choices. Promote it to planned work only when a concrete task or owner decision selects it. Do not silently implement permanent stat bonuses, multiplayer, breeding, or a large procedural ecosystem as part of the starter.
