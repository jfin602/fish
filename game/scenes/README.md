# P1 movement course

`main.tscn` instances `movement_course.tscn` under `Habitat/MovementCourse`. The saved course contains markers, three solid obstacles and visible native target/landmark meshes. `StarterWorld.cs` checks the four local starter resources, installs Fish3 as the player visual, places two Rock Face 01 meshes and two *Heliopora coerulea* meshes at the saved course markers, and applies Coast Sand 01 to the Fish-authored floor. The selected source geometry and textures remain in ignored `assets/vendor-local/`; neither saved scene embeds them. Seven other Quaternius fish are stationary decoration. The `FishController` owns movement and collision through the shared `SwimMotor`; `PlayShell` owns pause/display/input-mode presentation.

## Working scale and route

Coordinates are Godot metres, with the spawn at `(0, 0, 0)` and forward through the course toward negative Z. The floor and ceiling centers are Y `-4.2` and `4.2`; their surfaces are at approximately `-4.1` and `4.1`. The four invisible retaining walls are at X/Z `±9.5`, making a 19 m square habitat. The provisional controller clamps its center to X/Z `±8.3` and Y `±3`. The player collision is a sphere of radius `0.48` m (diameter `0.96` m). Imported Fish3 is visually scaled to `0.12`, about `0.95` m long, independently of that collision body, and its corrected nose faces the root's negative Z heading. `FollowCamera` keeps the rig in world space, centered 3.8 m behind and 2.0 m above at level spawn, looking 0.8 m ahead. The 68° vertical FOV and 4.5 s⁻¹ follow response are in `resources/swim_tuning.tres`; the view lags slightly during turns while obstacle retraction remains prompt. Mouse-mode pan defaults are 90°/s yaw and 65°/s elevation, limited to ±85° and ±35°, with a 5 s⁻¹ return response. Wheel zoom works in either mode, from 2.2 to 6.5 m at 0.4 m per notch; it stays for the session and resets on input-mode change. P10 owner acceptance is still open.

Each rock box is `2.724 × 1.96 × 2.105` m, offset `(0.189, 0.962, -0.854)` m from its visual root. Headless mesh-bound inspection found all six corresponding box faces within `0.001` m of the imported visual bounds. The horizontal passage is `2.476` m wide between collision faces, leaving `1.516` m beyond the fish diameter. The rocks meet the seabed; the center route at Y `-3` is open. Native collision simplifies the detailed rock silhouette deliberately. The coral and target spheres do not block movement.

The reproducible reset is to quit the test session from the pause panel and relaunch `./tools/dev run --display-mode=windowed-fullscreen` from the repository root. The saved main scene always starts at the spawn; no gameplay reset or persistent living-save system is present. Keyboard mode uses W thrust, S slow, A/D yaw and Up/Down pitch. Mouse mode uses relative movement to turn/pitch, left hold to swim, right hold to slow, and middle press or Esc for pause. In mouse mode W/S raise/lower and A/D orbit the camera without moving the fish. Each axis returns to the centered follow position when released; holding Shift keeps the adjusted view until Shift is released. The wheel zooms closer/up or farther/down in either input mode. Open Options by pointer or Esc to select the mode, sensitivity and inversion. For headless composition checks, use `./tools/dev check`, `./tools/dev inspect-scene game/scenes/movement_course.tscn`, and `./tools/dev inspect-course`. The latter checks real resource identities, shell presence, visual/marker placement, rock mesh/collision bounds and physics clearance at spawn, along the passage and at all four retaining walls. The composed check also exercises input lifecycle and camera obstruction/recovery in the saved scene. It does not establish native capture comfort, clipping-free rendered pixels, camera feel or swimming feel. [P5 evidence](../../docs/tasks/p1/qualification/native-display-and-assets.md) records the provisional native display and four-source observation; completed motor/camera/mode feel still needs P10.
