# FAF style proof 001 — integrated reef checkpoint

**Isolated owner-review candidate, 2026-11-10.** This is the first in-engine composition of the completed FAF fish, rock, coral, plant, terrain and detail base packs. It uses only their original GLBs and the existing Fish `UnderwaterAtmosphere` Forward+ presentation. The scene is deliberately separate from `main.tscn` and `p2_habitat.tscn`; it has no player motor, gameplay collision, ecology or movement changes. The [approved reef image](../references/reef-style-target.png) is the primary visual comparison. The checkpoint is ready for owner review; **FAF Art Style v1 is not ready for integrated production lock** on the evidence below.

## Open and review

- [Isolated scene](../../../game/scenes/faf_style_proof_001.tscn) and [review/capture script](../../../game/dev/faf_style_proof_001.gd). Run the scene in Godot 4.7.2 Forward+. Keys **1–7** select gameplay, fish close, arch, west reef, east reef, overview and overhead water views. The default is a static follow-scale camera approximately 3.9 m behind and 1.7 m above the Yellow Tang, at the accepted P1 distance/height and 75° lens. This checks composition from the intended perspective, but it is not an interactive swim/collision test.
- Native 1920 × 1200 views: [gameplay scale](evidence/gameplay.png), [fish close](evidence/fish_close.png), [arch](evidence/arch.png), [west reef](evidence/west_reef.png), [east reef](evidence/east_reef.png), [overview](evidence/overview.png), [surface](evidence/surface.png). These were captured on an AMD Radeon 880M with the current Fish Forward+ shaders and atmosphere, with eleven imported fish Swim Actions running.
- [Source-controlled composition builder](../../../tools/asset-factory/godot/build_style_proof_001.py) holds intentional placements and reads GLB terrain triangles to ground props on the highest overlapping surface. The [placement manifest](placement-manifest.json) records every transform and source triangle count. The saved `.tscn` contains real PackedScene instances, so the full authored composition is visible in the Godot editor without running its script. Rebuild only this proof with `python3 tools/asset-factory/godot/build_style_proof_001.py --root "$PWD" --overwrite`.

## Assets and composition

All **43 distinct FAF GLBs** appear at least once: five fish species, four rock variants, all twelve coral variants from four families, all eight plant variants from three families, all six terrain chunks, and all eight details. The scene holds **141 instances**: 11 fish, 19 rocks, 50 coral, 30 plants, 13 terrain and 18 details. It shares imported PackedScene meshes/material resources for repeated variants; no new fish, terrain or texture asset was authored. All eleven fish play their own `Swim` Action in place; no animation contains game translation.

The broad open-sand centre points toward an unequal three-boulder swim-through arch. The west side rises through the slope onto a shelf and low projecting ledge, with warm coral and grass around its stones. The east side uses rocky floor, a drop-off and stacked cliff/overhang masses; cyan, pink and purple coral sit between its green plant clumps. Small shells, rubble, pebbles and sponges are sparse beside larger forms. Distant Chromis, clownfish, butterflyfish and a far shark establish animal scale without filling the route. Terrain placements overlap and vary angle; the composition does not use a random scatter or regular grid.

For this isolated composition, the atmosphere receives the existing `WorldEnvironment`, one `Sun`, and non-rendering `Seabed`/upper-boundary anchors that its current initializer expects. The upper visual extent is the normal 84 m Fish size; the hidden depth anchor positions this small proof in the shallow lit part of the water column. No P2 seabed mesh or third-party art is rendered. Existing atmosphere code supplies its sky, surface shader, depth fog, single sun, organic caustic decal, one shaft mesh, 128 GPU motes and below-UI screen treatment. The different proof depth anchors mean this is the same rendering implementation under a fixture water column, not a literal P2 habitat camera sample.

## Direct visual comparison to the reef target

| Target principle | Observed in native proof | Qualification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Open swimming lane framed by busy reef | Clear centre, asymmetric west/east clusters, foreground color accents | Directionally good; lane is visually readable, but passage has no collision proof. |
| Broad natural rock arch and layered cliffs | Central opening and two raised reef masses are recognizable | Weak: the boulder lintel and stacked stones read assembled, with rounded repeated boulder profiles. No dedicated FAF arch, cliff or overhang mesh exists. |
| Continuous warm sand with elevation | Open floor, rolling patch, slope, shelf and drop-off appear | Weak: overlap apron edges and pale green/gray region changes are obvious at gameplay scale; overview exposes the finite diorama perimeter. |
| Lush varied coral, plants and fish | All families coexist; stronger warm/cool groups in the nearer reefs | Partial: forms remain sparse beside oversized stone masses. Branching Y shapes, rigid fans and radial tube clusters repeat. Plants can become narrow edge-on. |
| Bright sunlight, caustics and luminous blue water | Water surface and soft shafts are present; distant rock/coral share cool attenuation | Weak: the surface reads as a broad cyan sheet above a dark band, ground caustics are too faint to supply the reference's patterned light, and the overall sand/stone field is flatter and cooler. The isolated lighting retained Fish's Natural settings. |
| Recognizable fish at the actual follow distance | Close view shows the Tang and clownfish markings | Weak: the 0.237 m Tang is a tiny rear silhouette from the accepted 3.8 m follow distance; Chromis largely disappear and the distant shark is only a small accent. This is a camera/species scale decision, not a reason to silently enlarge the source fish. |

The graphic low-poly dialects are compatible: faceted rocks, chunky coral, broad plant blades and simple fish materials look like related assets. The scene still lacks the reference's dramatic continuous arch, terrain integration, luminous sun, strong caustic pattern and lush depth layering. Simple geometry has gained some richness from clustering and the existing water haze, but lighting does not yet carry enough of the finish.

## Technical observation

The [manifest](placement-manifest.json) sums **45,922 authored source triangles** across all placements: terrain 1,664; rocks 4,294; coral 25,290; plants 5,812; details 1,250; fish 7,612. There are 252 placed mesh primitive surfaces before visibility/batching and 41 distinct material names across the source GLBs. The fish account for 113 of those surfaces because each rigged species has multiple meshes/marking regions. Coral contributes most triangles and 50 instances. The eight detail types remain cheap and do not stand out as a hotspot.

In one native 1920 × 1200 Forward+ capture run, Godot reported 162 draw calls / 48,862 rendered primitives for the gameplay view, 133 / 30,094 for fish close, 155 / 33,296 for the arch, 135 / 37,438 west, 115 / 36,042 east, 165 / 52,194 overview, and 5 / 11,150 for the overhead surface. These are one-frame observations that include the Fish water pass, shaft/surface meshes, shadow work and visible FAF instances; they are not a frame-time budget or production density acceptance. The 84 m water surface alone carries its existing subdivided geometry. No full optimization campaign was run.

## Refinement before production scaling

1. **Rock family:** make dedicated arch/overhang/cliff modules or a more structural rock construction path. The four boulder variants are useful fillers, but stacking/scaling them does not create the reference's natural landmark.
2. **Terrain family:** improve multi-chunk transitions, perimeter shape and sand/reef color continuity. The current overlap aprons are visibly patch-like from the accepted camera; more plants/details cannot conceal every edge.
3. **Water/light review:** compare the existing Natural lighting against the reference with the owner. The surface/caustic strength and pale green sand read need a dedicated, bounded presentation decision; this proof changed no production rendering values.
4. **Fish presentation:** decide what the eventual player species and follow-camera scale imply for a sub-0.25 m fish. Chromis contrast and far-view size also need scene-level review before schooling density is set.
5. **Repetition:** broaden branching/fan coral silhouettes and rotate/interleave plant clumps deliberately. Keep detail density sparse; small details vanish at longer distance as intended. If many fish remain visible, the multiple rigged mesh surfaces may merit a focused material/draw review.

**Decision:** technically integrated and suitable as the first owner visual checkpoint. The scene does **not** pass the FAF native style checklist or lock Art Style v1 for production scaling. The approved *direction* remains valid; its first full composition exposes specific asset and lighting gaps. Owner review can use the linked views to decide whether those gaps match the intended Fish game. No FAF asset was placed in the live P2 world and no Roadmap Gate was changed.
