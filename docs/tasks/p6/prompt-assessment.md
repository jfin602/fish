## Why P6 now

The P5 isolated Pike encounter proved a narrow stealth/feeding/growth foundation, and owner acceptance closed P5 **for progression / NOT GREEN**. C5 changes the player to Bass, adds hatching, early food, Bluegill schools, Pike targeting and automatic mouth feeding/Spacebar boost. The owner observes too little risk or exploration through the early growth stages: repeatedly collecting close fixed food is sufficient until late Pike contact, and aiming at tiny food is frustrating without a consistent reference. Persisting such a shallow loop first would preserve too little meaningful gameplay. **S27 explicitly replaces the next Phase with P6 Living Pond; persistence, Life History and integration move to P7–P9.**

## Dependencies, allowed reuse and explicit deferrals

Keep Forward+ and the existing water optics, dynamic waterline, fish/camera interpolation, C5 wake, SFX/audio, boost and input lifecycle. Do not introduce hidden changes to their simulation semantics. New Pond wildlife uses only Pond-native FAF. Do not overwrite source asset exports, pinned bakes or the Ocean roster. Real 3D sensing depends on line of sight, actual occlusion and finite local movement cues, not gameplay camera, reticle or screen fog. Hatchling catches/retry are **ephemeral only** in P6; durable save, anti-resurrection, run archive (P7), recount/history (P8), and final multi-session/platform qualification (P9) are deliberately deferred. No whole-pond scientific food web, frog deep-water pursuer, automatic prey magnetism, unlimited food spawn, arbitrary permanent hunger meter or guaranteed school-distraction mechanic.
