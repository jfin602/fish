TASK: Phase 2 / P4 — Reef tunnels and world composition

MODEL / REASONING / USAGE
- Recommended configuration: `GPT-6 Sol High`.
- Browser required: yes.
- This prompt's assigned project version is `0.2.4`.

GOAL
Turn the structural P2 habitat into a deliberate reef exploration space with coral floor, varied rock formations, a tunnel/tight passage, cover/overhang, open cruising space and meaningful vertical routes. Remove obvious developer-course visuals from normal play. This task includes an owner/native visual checkpoint before the composition is accepted.

PREREQUISITES AND NARROW READING
Read AGENTS.md, BOOT.md, stack README, implementation-plan.md P4, P2 design asset/world sections, art-and-assets P2 boundary, roadmap G2.1 and V18. Inspect actual P3 habitat, final P1 fish/camera dimensions/tuning, Rock Face/Coast Sand/Heliopora prepared resources, current ambient fish and asset provenance. Require root `0.2.3`.

IMPLEMENT
1. Compose at least three readable traversal categories: broad/open cruising, a tighter rock tunnel/arch/cave-like passage, and a vertical route between lower/upper landmarks. Add one overhang or sheltered pocket that demonstrates cover without trapping the camera.
2. Use rock instances with varied transform/scale/orientation and compatible simplified collision to form natural walls, tunnel edges and landmarks. Collision may be simpler than visual geometry, but the fish/camera clearance must not promise space that physics rejects.
3. Build coral/reef coverage across parts of the seabed so it reads as a coral floor/reef zone rather than two isolated specimens. Treat the high-detail Heliopora as a measured resource: use sparse hero instances, optimized derivatives, lower-cost authored pieces, LOD/instancing or additional approved free assets as needed. Do not densely clone the raw specimen by default.
4. Keep areas of visual rest/open sand so the scene is not uniformly cluttered. Retain recognizable depth landmarks and sightlines for the third-person camera.
5. Ensure normal play has no visible P1 target spheres, camera-check cylinder, flat second-ground illusion or exposed test-wall look. Development-only fixtures can retain diagnostic shapes outside normal play.
6. If new third-party environment assets are truly required, use only free legally suitable sources with complete provenance/license/dependency records and public-repo-safe installation. Do not add paid/account-gated dependencies or unresolved third-party textures.
7. Extend scene/resource/collision checks to cover tunnel centerline/clearance, overhang route, broad route, vertical landmarks, no spawn overlaps, bounded representative instance counts and source identities. Record implementation evidence and any preparation changes.

OWNER VISUAL CHECKPOINT
After the implementation and focused checks are green, launch the actual normal Fish game in a native Godot window and hand control to the owner before accepting this task. Do not use a still editor viewport as the substitute.

The owner should swim:
- one broad/open route;
- the intended rock tunnel/tight passage;
- the overhang/sheltered pocket;
- the lower-to-upper vertical route;
- enough ordinary free swimming to see the habitat from several angles.

Ask for feedback on the actual composition, especially:
- whether the world already feels like terrain rather than arranged test props;
- whether the coral floor/reef coverage feels natural or repetitive;
- whether rock scale/orientation/spacing feels believable;
- whether the tunnel and overhang are visually inviting and physically readable;
- whether the third-person camera has enough room;
- whether obvious flat/test-course geometry is still visible;
- whether any area feels too empty, too cluttered or copy-pasted.

Use that feedback to make bounded P4 composition/collision adjustments and replay only the affected route(s). This checkpoint judges layout, scale, traversal and visual composition; it does not require final underwater color/fog/caustics, which P5 owns. Record the owner's accepted composition or exact remaining complaint in the P2 implementation evidence. Do not proceed to P5 on a composition the owner explicitly rejects.

VALIDATE

Automated geometry success does not replace the owner/native composition review.
