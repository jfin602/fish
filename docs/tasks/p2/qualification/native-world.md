## Repairs made during P7

- Native spawn, open route and sheltered-pocket captures showed square, high-contrast boundaries between the four seabed material regions. `SeabedSurface.cs` now builds the same continuous collidable heightfield as one visual surface with `seabed_blend.gdshader`. Coast Sand 01, Ground060, Gravel041 and Rock063 color textures blend by world position; the dominant material supplies normal and roughness maps. This removed the visible square seam in repeat native captures. The collision mesh, basin height function and prepared asset identities did not change.
- A turn capture showed hard square bubble sprites. `WaterReaction.cs` now draws bounded spherical bubbles, with ribbon width `0.06 m` and ribbon alpha `0.30`; amount, ratio mapping, lifetime, emission bounds, fish-space offsets and movement ownership remain the P6 values. The repeat native capture shows round bubbles. Owner judgment of wake readability and clutter remains pending.
- A temporary denser-fog trial (`0.012` distance, `0.032` volumetric) offered little improvement at the near rim and reduced the open-route sample. It was not saved. The tracked P5 atmosphere remains `0.0035` distance and `0.024` volumetric density.
