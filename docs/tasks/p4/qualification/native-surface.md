### Diagnosis and implementation

R1 already samples the pre-surface opaque scene and depth buffer on the Pond's corrected air-facing triangles. Its `0.22` per-meter depth estimate used the difference in view-space Z, then raised surface alpha from roughly `0.14` toward `0.86`. Because alpha stayed below one and the surface color still included the original scene, substantial fish, log and bottom color survived at depth. View-space Z also underestimates the optical path of an oblique ray. The R1 Pond air camera uses air fog and zero underwater volume/height fog; the surface was not receiving the full underwater fog a second time. The vertex-shader wave changes the actual `surface_world` entry point; shoreline clipping, edge fade and the shore mask remain in place. A separate pale R2 trial proved that replacing deep scene contribution with a light green scattering color fixes exposure of the bottom but creates a flat, bright sheet.

The air branch now samples color and opaque depth at the same refracted UV. It reconstructs view-space entry at `FRAGCOORD.z` and the opaque target at sampled depth, transforms the target to world space, and applies water absorption only if the target lies below the waved surface. The optical length is `d = distance(entry_view, target_view)`, so oblique views have a longer path than near-vertical ones. Open water uses a 30 m path; an opaque target in air bypasses the surface color. The Pond-only model is `T = exp(-0.65 * d / daylight)`. `daylight` is clamped `SunEnergy / 1.35` in `[0.6, 1.3]`, so the default Overcast setting gives `1.0` and Brighter Shallows' `1.65` sun energy gives about `1.22`: the latter increases transmission without whitening the surface. Missing depth and grazing rays remain bounded by exponential decay and the clamp. At straight paths of 0.5/1/2/3/4 m, the default mathematical transmission is about 72/52/27/14/7%; these figures are a model, **not** native acceptance evidence.

Lost contribution blends toward Pond-only muddy olive `Color(0.055, 0.085, 0.035)`. The direct water tint is reduced to `0.01 + 0.04 * Fresnel`, and distant air haze approaches the same dark water color instead of the pale gray air fog color. The sky and above-water bank still use the existing air atmosphere. The underwater branch, Pond near-field fog/light, R1 triangle winding, fish transforms/collider, Ocean profile and screen/UI shader are unchanged. Ocean's air-facing surface remains disabled and its extinction uniform is zero. The opaque depth buffer cannot classify translucent geometry that does not write depth; moving fish, crossings and complicated vegetation overlap need native route review.
