## Baseline and diagnosis

The baseline Sun was a single `DirectionalLight3D` with `shadow_enabled=false`. Its direct energy and ambient were adjusted from **camera depth**, so near wood and plants had directional material response but no true cast shadow, and the terrain could look uniformly exposed. The overhead water underside was an even color field. At the noon log pose, [controlled isolation](diagnostics/lighting-isolation.png) shows that removing direct light darkens the fish, bark rim and mud, while removing ambient makes the nearby log too black to navigate. Removing the existing two overcast shafts or caustic decal barely changes that view. Switching on default multi-split shadows makes real contact shade, but raised the same-pose draw count from 210 to 568 and one measured median from 4.10 to 11.53 ms. A single orthogonal map, 28 m range, with the transparent surface excluded as a caster reduced the experimental map to 294 draws; [shadow-cost captures and timings](diagnostics/shadow-cost/manifest.json) show the test. This is a visual/cost tradeoff, not a claim of cheap shadows.

The [initial surface trial](diagnostics/overbright-surface-trial.png) produced bright perspective bands and was rejected within R3. It was replaced by nearby, lower-energy surface breaks. The trial is not the candidate.

## Lighting candidate

The existing Pond Sun now casts one bounded orthogonal shadow map in daylight, with no new light node. The water plane does not cast a solid shadow. Sun direction, warmth, energy, shaft lean and night behavior continue to come from the c4 shared `EnvironmentClock`/`SolarState`; shadows turn off when direct sunlight falls below the defined threshold. Pond basin color gets soft projected sunlight pools that follow the solar direction and the existing presentation time, and decay as `exp(-0.16 × actual water depth)`. The water underside gets nearby, restrained daylight breaks from its existing wave phase and sun color; its distant field remains subdued and the air-facing reflection/transmission branch is unchanged. The existing two overcast shaft ribbons keep one mesh/draw but their alpha edges wander and break irregularly. Caustic decal energy and the R2 optical fog, absorption, local sediment texture/density and preset settings remain unchanged. These are visual-only changes; no fish, camera, clock, collision, or saved state writes were added.

At noon, this run measured **9.52 / 17.15** baseline versus **6.58 / 10.64 ms** candidate; the apparent improvement conflicts with the added shadow work and the 17:00 result, so it is treated as host/run variation, not a speed gain. R2 previously measured **4.98 / 8.48 ms** for its noon candidate route at 202 draws/182,240 primitives. Its separate run and earlier probe are context, not a directly matched R3 benchmark. Candidate p95 here stayed below 16.67 ms on both routes, but repeated native play is still needed for frame-pacing judgment.

## Remaining work and R4 direction

The existing camera-depth scalar still dims the single DirectionalLight uniformly for imported wood/vegetation at a given view; R3's true world-depth fade is on the Pond basin and light effects, not every material. A future material-level treatment would need to preserve FAF textures/normals and demonstrate its cost. The shadow map's added draws/pacing require owner-platform review, especially under low sun and while crossing the waterline. The broad distant underside, far bank transition, subtle shafts/caustics and dark log interior remain visual limits.

For R4, hold this lighting/solar candidate fixed while comparing the owner's images and same-depth clear bank, ordinary water, silty pocket and shaded cover. Adjust the seeded sediment field and optical density locally only where evidence shows insufficient separation, keeping night darkness in illumination rather than adding fog. Owner acceptance of R2/R3, a continuous native swim, cross-waterline view and P12/V32 judgment remain open. No commit, push, version bump or Gate pass is claimed.
