The old normalized field had a broad `0.31` base, plus 3D noise, a shallow subtraction, near-bed term and weak shoreline/shelter term. Texture filtering and `0.25` volume edge fade further smooth a 0.95 m vertical / about 2 m horizontal voxel spacing. The world anchor is the volume center, not the moving camera; the local density was restored after every diagnostic. A numeric noise range alone was therefore insufficient evidence of visible spatial clarity.

## Implemented field

`PondTurbidityField` now uses a seeded **2D broad pocket** to keep clear/silty regions coherent through the water column, a smaller seeded 3D detail term, an independently weighted near-bed addition, and a modest depth/shelter contribution. Shallow water can be clearer, but specific silty shallows still exist. Smooth masks fade density near bed, shore and surface; no spherical fog nodes, fish clouds, new draw pass or camera-driven field were added. The normalized clamp is `0.025–0.95`, and the single FogMaterial scale is `0.14`. The texture remains 64×16×64 R8 (**65,536 raw texel bytes**; actual GPU allocation/volumetric buffer overhead was not exposed). The existing FogVolume box, its world anchor, edge fade, Pond world bake and global optical/light models are unchanged. Sediment sampling takes no solar/clock/illumination input.

The [side-by-side field visualization](diagnostics/field-comparison.png) shows broad, gradual clear and silty areas at the same 2 m depth. Eligible grid samples now span `0.050–0.732` in the quantized, filtered texture; the focused continuous-field check samples `0.025–0.743` directly before texture quantization. The maximum comparable-depth gap in that check is `0.705`. At the **fixed C pair**, C1 `(18, −12)` has water depth `5.15 m`, and C2 `(−27, −36)` has `5.61 m`; the depth difference is `0.46 m`. Their fixed camera samples change from baseline `0.225/0.244` to candidate `0.067/0.703`. At the near-bed camera positions these become `0.091/0.784` in the candidate. The former B baseline pair `(-30,-27)` and `(3,9)` was recaptured without moving it; its new contrast is weaker. This is a relocated broad sediment pattern, not a claim that every prior muddy point remains muddy.

## Remaining visual problems and R5

The local contrast is visible in the chosen silty pocket but still modest in the full composition, especially where the large smooth water underside, distance fog and low-contrast terrain dominate. The old B pair is no longer a strong clear/silty pair, and a field map does not prove all routes feel natural. The 16-slice vertical field cannot express thin bed plumes; no extra treatment was justified by this evidence. The R3 daylight-shadow cost and owner judgment remain open, as do full continuous play, repeated waterline crossing, other presets/times, and the owner's reference-image comparison. R5 should address **environment readability** through nearby mud/wood/vegetation contrast, distant silhouette and overhead/bank transitions in native motion, keeping sediment and illumination independent. Review C1/C2 and the normal route in native play before any visual acceptance or Gate decision.
