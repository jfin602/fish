## Diagnosis

The [baseline optics/light isolation](baseline/isolation-matrix.png) turns off all water fog or direct sunlight at F1/F3/F5/F6 without moving the camera. With fog off, distant geometry emerges, but even the selected dense area still has isolated small underwater algae and broad exposed mud. Direct light off makes wood and ground darker, but light alone does not explain missing near/mid plant layers. The sampled F3 cluster has `FAF-PLANT-008A/B`, reeds and details nearby in world space; those counts do not translate to a strong foreground silhouette from the fish camera. F4/F10 negative space is useful, while F3 and F6 lack the deliberate asymmetrical vegetation enclosure seen in the reference. Procedural placement repeats small pieces over a broad area rather than building a few high-impact layered clusters.

The imported wood has named opaque dark bark, ochre bark, warm break and olive algae materials on low-poly facets. It retains the correct hollow and collision, but near F1 much of its broad surface reads as one dark brown mass; the reference has stronger, selective facet value shifts and natural moss on a more complex root silhouette. The plant surfaces are opaque, so their weak separation is due to small geometry, similar green backgrounds and daylight/fog response, not transparent overdraw. The basin has broad silt/mud colors and R3 solar pools, but its existing value field is subdued and the F5 slope looks nearly uniform. No normal map is present in these simple low-poly assets; arbitrary normal noise would work against their authored faceting. Existing direct sunlight creates some contact/shadow difference; [direct-off captures](baseline/isolation/F1_close_wood_direct_off.png) show its contribution, but the scene's sparse geometry limits what those shadows can reveal. These are material and composition findings alongside the inherited optical limits, not a reason to clear the water.

## Implemented candidate and visible result

`PondFafEnvironment` now duplicates imported StandardMaterial3D resources into **shared scene-local instances** for structural wood surfaces and plant MultiMesh parts. Opaque albedo factors distinguish dark bark, ochre facets, warm breaks, olive algae and subdued greens; wood roughness is capped at `0.90` for a small light response. Materials are cached by original resource and family so repeated pieces share a material. No texture, transparent overlay, normal perturbation, collision, placement or FAF source file was added. The Pond basin shader gains a broad skewed world-space alluvial value pattern; it retains R3's solar pools and rough mud response. The world remains the same baked 1,101-placement Pond.

The [candidate contact sheet](candidate/contact-sheet.png) and [all matched pairs](comparison/) show a **modest** increase in close bark facet separation and some green/ochre distinction. [Fish detail](comparison/fish-detail.png), [wood facets](comparison/wood-facets.png), [sunlit plants](comparison/sunlit-plants.png), [shaded wood](comparison/shaded-wood.png), [mud and plants](comparison/mud-and-plants.png), and [depth layers](comparison/depth-layers.png) preserve the full context and useful crops. Full-frame mean absolute RGB change is only `1.30–3.47/255` across the 11 poses; it is an image-difference measure, not an artistic score. F1 changes most; F3 vegetation and F5 mud remain underwhelming. The hero entrance remains readable, but the R5 scene still does not approach the reference's dense stems, root arches, overhead pads or rich near/mid/far layering. The [candidate motion sheet](candidate/motion-sheet.png) and [route manifest](candidate/motion/manifest.json) show no obvious new pop or route obstruction in sampled frames, but cannot establish human swimming feel or the whole continuous route.

### Isolated composition test, not adopted

The probe's `r5-visual-only-71` test adds 19 deterministic existing-FAF root, stick, stone and algae pieces near F3/F6 only after loading the accepted bake. The [manifest](diagnostics/prototype/manifest.json) lists every x/z, bed height, yaw, scale and FAF ID and declares `bake_changed=false`, `collision_added=false`. [F3](comparison/F3_dense-prototype.png) and [F6](comparison/F6_sunlit_plants-prototype.png) show that simply stamping these available pieces produces oversized angular sticks and still little plant layering. This is a **negative design finding**, not a qualified bake proposal. It added 28 draws and 4,092 primitives at F3 (246/294,674 → 274/298,766) and has no collision, support-footprint, cover-network or route qualification. Do not copy its placements into the curated world. R6 should design an actual deterministic bake candidate with appropriate plant scale/density and root relationships, then validate support, routes, collision and cost before promotion.

## Performance and regression evidence

[Full fixed-view and route metrics](comparison/metrics.json) are uncapped native elapsed frame intervals, **not GPU-only time**. Sharing material instances brought all 11 fixed poses and the moving route back to **exactly the baseline draw and primitive counts**. R5 baseline→candidate examples: ordinary F0 286 draws/325,134 primitives, median/p95 `5.444/7.776 → 5.078/7.312 ms`; close wood F1 301/324,768, `4.815/8.699 → 4.652/7.516 ms`; F3 dense 246/294,674, `5.344/9.043 → 4.869/8.457 ms`; F6 sunlit 246/294,674, `4.764/6.981 → 5.198/8.009 ms`. Individual 90-frame tails fluctuate in both directions. The 8-second route was `6.139/9.340 ms` baseline versus `6.449/10.374 ms` candidate, each 282 draws/352,918 primitives; a [repeat](diagnostics/motion-repeat/manifest.json) was `6.185/10.089 ms`, 282 draws and 352,842 median primitives. Endpoints match to the reported precision, while per-second camera positions differ by at most one input tick. Relative to R4's `6.113/8.945 ms` route candidate, R5 does not establish a repeatable large cost regression, but p95 remains somewhat worse and merits owner/native observation. R1 original route was `4.737/6.093 ms`, 202 draws/182,240 primitives; the cumulative R2–R4 visual stack is materially costlier than original, and R5 does not reduce that inherited cost. The first uncached R5 capture's extra draws were corrected before final evidence. No overdraw pass or new geometry enters normal play.

[Import](diagnostics/import.log) and [build](diagnostics/build.log) passed; final build reports zero warnings and errors. Focused [scenery](diagnostics/inspect-pond-scenery.log), [camera immersion](diagnostics/inspect-pond-camera-immersion.log), [surface](diagnostics/inspect-pond-surface.log), [Pond/Ocean atmosphere](diagnostics/inspect-pond-atmosphere.log), [solar day/night](diagnostics/inspect-solar-lighting.log), and [freshwater creatures](diagnostics/inspect-pond-creatures.log) checks passed. Scenery reports 31/32 camera/fish-clearance samples with one *existing authored obstruction*; R5 adds no collision or placement. The solar check covers noon/night and Ocean/Pond isolation; no R5 night image was captured. The creature check loads five species and 25 stages, but R5 native screenshots show only Bluegill, so other fish appearance in motion remains unobserved. Baseline/candidate native logs have no Godot shader or runtime error marker. Open-editor manual control and an owner continuous swim were not observed.

## Closing decision

**Needs R6, then owner qualification.** R5 improves local material separation cheaply and preserves the curated bake, but the underwater Pond still lacks the intentional near/mid/far vegetation, overhead canopy and substantial branching wood that make the owner's reference compelling. R6 should first prototype a small number of authored composition sites using suitable FAF assets, preserve open swim lanes, and bake them under a new explicit candidate identity only after support/collision/route checks. If the current asset kit cannot make those layers without giant sticks or repetitive algae, request a focused asset-kit extension rather than weakening water absorption or falsely calling R5 visually accepted. The [cross-iteration review](../visual-progress.md) tracks what changed and what remains unobserved. P12/V32, c4 and root version remain unchanged pending owner native review.
