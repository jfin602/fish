## Diagnosis

The [baseline isolation matrix](diagnostics/diagnostic-matrix.png) holds the same Bluegill, camera, world and noon illumination. [Full effect](diagnostics/D0_full.png) has a large, smooth olive field above dark mud. [Local turbidity off](diagnostics/D1_local_off.png) makes the log and background vegetation modestly clearer. [Distance fog off](diagnostics/D2_distance_off.png) changes the near scene less. [Height fog off](diagnostics/D3_height_off.png) is nearly indistinguishable here. [Global and local volume off](diagnostics/D4_volume_off.png) reveals many more distant silhouettes, showing that the two volume contributions accumulate strongly. [Screen treatment off](diagnostics/D5_screen_off.png) changes little, consistent with the 0.04 px warp and very small color variation. [Combined global fog reduction](diagnostics/D6_combined_reduction.png) gives a modest gain without a hard horizon. [Restored](diagnostics/D7_restored.png) returns to the full effect. The diagnostic changes were temporary and restored between comparisons.

Additional [candidate lighting/effects matrix](diagnostics/candidate/lighting-effects-matrix.png) shows that increasing ambient 35% lifts nearby objects slightly, removing direct light darkens fish and mud markedly, and removing shafts/caustics/motes mainly removes local cues. Lighting is therefore important to structure but was not the main cause of the broad haze. Source inspection and A4–A7 show that the 3 m distance-fog start, 18 m Overcast end, depth-shortened range, height fog, Pond water colors and sky all remain active. The screen pass performs one linearly filtered screen sample with subpixel warp; it is not a full-image blur filter. The biggest observed flattening comes from cumulative volumetric scatter with a broad local field, on top of dark olive distance and background colors. The fixed camera cannot fully separate every overlapping effect; these conclusions are limited to the captured views.

## One focused change and result

Pond-only global volumetric multiplier changed `0.72 → 0.38`. The seeded 64×16×64 field kept one FogVolume and its world placement; its broad base changed `0.43 → 0.31`, shallow suppression `0.12 → 0.15`, and near-bed term `0.23 → 0.34`. This leaves less suspended material in ordinary water and a stronger relative bed contribution. The 3 m distance-fog start, 18/24 m preset range, `0.58` Pond distance extinction, Ocean optics, lighting, surface reflection, screen shader, fish and world geometry were not changed by R1.

The [ordinary-water pair](comparison/A2_near-pair.png), [shallow pair](comparison/A4_shallow-pair.png) and [middle pair](comparison/A6_middle-pair.png) reveal a little more log, plant and floor contrast. The [log](comparison/A3_log-pair.png) and [deep](comparison/A7_deep-pair.png) remain dim. The fish body and fins remain visible at the sampled distance, but close fish detail did not materially improve. The broad green field remains prominent, and the 10-view full-image mean absolute change ranges only about 1–6/255 per color channel. [Above water](comparison/A10_air-pair.png) is pixel-identical. This is a real but small optical change, not an accepted artistic repair.

## Performance and checks

The [benchmark table](comparison/benchmarks.md) gives 90-frame warmed fixed-view medians/p95, draw calls and primitive counts, plus 8-second real keyboard-thrust route statistics. The two motion runs ended at exactly the same fish position after a fixed 36-physics-frame turn. On the route, median/p95 frame time was **4.74/6.09 ms before** and **4.73/6.16 ms after**; both had 202 median draws and 182,240 median primitives. Candidate local turbidity on/off at A2 measured 4.39/5.61 versus 4.35/5.62 ms, too close to establish a repeatable cost. The unchanged R8 texture is 64 KiB; no additional volume, texture, pass, draw or shader was added. Native GPU-only timing was unavailable. Baseline/candidate world load reported 1,554/1,353 ms and field texture creation 29.93/19.20 ms, values subject to startup/cache variation and not evidence of a load improvement.

The fixed baseline was captured before the implementation change. For the moving baseline, the two original Pond constants were temporarily restored exactly, rebuilt, and then reapplied to the candidate after capture; the existing c4 edits stayed in place. The motion probe uses the same baked world, selected fish, input events and fixed turn length in both runs. The matched endpoint and logged transforms make this a reproducible route comparison rather than a claim of manually identical frame timing.

`tools/dev import` and repeated .NET builds passed with zero warnings/errors. After the candidate, `inspect-pond-atmosphere` passed Pond→Ocean→Pond profile isolation, deterministic field continuity, fixed world anchor, diagnostic restoration and unchanged fish state; sampled field range was `0.100–0.457` over 191 normal-water points. `inspect-pond-surface`, `inspect-pond-camera-immersion` and `inspect-solar-lighting` passed. The 10 fixed native views, candidate on/off diagnostics and moving route logs contain no `ERROR`, `WARNING` or shader failure markers. The bake ID and content hash stayed identical. The fixed above-water comparison is identical, supporting preservation of the existing reflection appearance at that pose. The c4 day/night logic and shaders were untouched by R1; the solar tests passed, but a full native day/night cycle was not repeated here.

## Limits and next decision

Fixed poses and one normal-control route do not establish owner camera comfort, a full hollow-trunk swim, repeated waterline crossings, every GFX preset or every time of day. The fresh editor did not restore the current MCP server bridge, so editor-probe qualification remains unavailable. The route has consecutive timestamped frames, not video. The fixed-view benchmarks are elapsed frame intervals on this adapter, not isolated GPU timestamps. No owner judgment of this R1 appearance has been recorded.

**Recommendation for R2:** keep this packet as the comparison baseline and investigate the remaining flat green background color and near/far separation directly. Use a controlled color/absorption comparison that keeps the fish and near wood readable while darkening or shifting only distant water, then recapture the same poses. Do not mark P12 or c4 green from R1.
