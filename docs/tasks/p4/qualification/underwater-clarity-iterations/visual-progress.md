# Pond underwater visual progress — R1 through R5

**Review date:** 2026-11-12. **Status:** visual candidates, not owner acceptance. P12/V32 and c4 remain FAIL / NOT GREEN; root version is `0.4.11`. The owner approved the earlier air-side muddy-green look but found underwater too clear. The two underwater images described in the later R2–R5 task attachments were not files in those attachments, so the repository's [owner-supplied Pond style anchor](../../../../fish-asset-factory/references/pond/pond-style-target.png) and its explicitly aspirational [reference pack](../../../../fish-asset-factory/references/pond/README.md) guide the similarity discussion; no exact photographic matching or owner approval is claimed.

## Matched history

[A2 ordinary Bluegill and hollow trunk](r5/comparison/history-A2_near.png) and [A3 hollow entrance](r5/comparison/history-A3_log.png) compare R1 original, R2 optical candidate, R3 noon lighting candidate, R4 sediment candidate and R5 environment candidate. Both strips use the **same seed 71, baked world, fish transform, camera transform, noon time and 1920×1200 native Forward+ viewport**. Rendering changes accumulate, so later images include prior unapproved candidates. R4/R5 also include [11 identical fixed views and metrics](r5/comparison/metrics.json), plus [R5 before](r5/baseline/contact-sheet.png) / [after](r5/candidate/contact-sheet.png) contact sheets. The R5 F1/F3/F5/F6/F7/F8 extra views have no exact R1 camera equivalent; do not infer R1 pixel differences from them. R1→R5 route benchmarks are context across separate runs; R4→R5 is the directly matched local comparison.

**Decision:** Recommend a focused R6 composition correction under a separate deterministic bake candidate, with support/collision/route and native cost checks, then owner native qualification. Do not clear the water or treat four subsequent iterations as acceptance. Preserve R1's original images and earlier failed V32 evidence as historical facts.
