Candidates are scored against their intended depth band and location, require at least 13 m of shore margin, avoid geology centerlines and existing anchors, and pass fish/camera collision probes in normal generation. The spawn is selected from a shallow shelf, saved in the route data and restored to the playable Spawn marker on a `c3-full-9` baked load. Historical `c3-full-1`, `c3-full-3` and `c3-full-8` packages retain load support.

The 31-node, 37-edge graph connects spawn, shallow, middle, deep, trench, two open-water branches, two shallow returns, landmarks, and both ends of all five M2 geological passages. Every tunnel, including the east swim-through, has an entry approach, protected passage, far-side departure and onward connector to a **different** network hub. The stone arch's previously blocked `link-out` was repaired. A bounded, coarse 2D search with a curved height profile handles obstructed connectors before the slower 3D fallback; repaired routes avoid the former straight vertical legs. The planner samples open connectors every 0.3 m, leaves a conservative margin above the triangulated seabed and allows fish/camera clearance up to the actual water column. The hidden upper collision slab for the new basin was moved above the water surface; the historical Base World keeps its prior position.

The M4 protected-route inputs are the baked `world-data.json` `Nodes` and `Edges`: stable node IDs/positions/roles, edge IDs/endpoints/kinds/polyline points, `ProtectedRadius` (2.2 m open, 1.0 m enclosed), `CameraProtectedRadius` (0.65 m), and 2.0 m camera offset. `GeneratedWorldRoutes.IsProtected` reserves both swept corridors before dressing.
