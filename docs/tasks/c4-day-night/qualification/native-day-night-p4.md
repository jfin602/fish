## Follow-up — owner-reported pale shoreline at sunset

The owner supplied [this native screenshot](native-day-night-evidence/owner-shore-white.png) and reported that the shore turns white during sunset. This is an actual owner visual concern, not acceptance of the cycle. The file named `shader-error.png` was copied byte-for-byte as evidence; the observed issue was pale shore rendering, and the successful native logs did not report a shader compilation error.

The [fixed far-shore baseline](native-day-night-evidence/shore-white-before.log) reproduced the bright strip at 18:00, 18:30 and 00:00. It showed Pond air fog color fixed at `(0.43, 0.48, 0.46)` across noon through night, while sky horizon color fell from `(0.38, 0.56, 0.61)` at noon to `(0.008, 0.019, 0.038)` at night. The static distance-fog color explained why distant shore geometry stayed pale against the dark sky.

The repair makes air fog color follow the shared solar horizon as daylight falls. In the measured Pond view, fog density stays `0.940` above water; the existing underwater fog and Pond/Ocean optical profiles remain unchanged. The [matched native after log](native-day-night-evidence/shore-white-after.log) shows fog color `(0.43, 0.48, 0.46)` at noon, `(0.1516, 0.2175, 0.2435)` at 18:00, `(0.0474, 0.0763, 0.0985)` at 18:30 and `(0.008, 0.019, 0.038)` at midnight. Noon [before](native-day-night-evidence/before_pond_far_shore_1200.jpg) and [after](native-day-night-evidence/after_pond_far_shore_1200.jpg) look unchanged. The pale strip in the 18:30 [before](native-day-night-evidence/before_pond_far_shore_1850.jpg) is absent in the [after](native-day-night-evidence/after_pond_far_shore_1850.jpg); the 18:00 [before](native-day-night-evidence/before_pond_far_shore_1800.jpg) and [after](native-day-night-evidence/after_pond_far_shore_1800.jpg) show the same direction. A fixed image region `(100,555)–(1800,585)` had mean 8-bit luminance `71.7 → 15.0` at 18:30 and `77.0 → 40.5` at 18:00; noon stayed `107.9 → 107.9`. These pixel samples quantify only that diagnostic view.
