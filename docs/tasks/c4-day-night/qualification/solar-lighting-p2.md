# C4 day/night P2 — solar sky and lighting

**2026-11-12.** Direct implementation from clean `main` at `7814e9199ce1fd6957ae09a0b4549810f9fa070e`, root `0.4.11`. This working-tree candidate has no self-commit or push. P3 water/underwater response and P4 native visual acceptance remain open; P4 P12 remains NOT GREEN.

## Implemented

The existing `underwater_sky.gdshader` and its one `Sky`/`ShaderMaterial` are reused. The air side now receives continuously varying Pond/Ocean horizon and zenith colors, a smooth vertical gradient, localized low-sun warmth and a bounded solar disc/glow. The old air-side static cloud pattern was removed; the Pond `overcast` uniform remains only as transitional underwater compatibility. No weather state was added.

`SolarLightingState` maps P1's `SolarState` to profile-specific sky, direct-light and ambient values without owning time or renderer resources. `UnderwaterAtmosphere` points the existing `DirectionalLight3D` toward P1's sun every frame, updates its color/energy and volumetric contribution, and updates the Environment ambient color/energy. A small nighttime ambient floor keeps the air-side lighting above black. The clock processes before its presentation consumer. The old fixed sun pitch/yaw controls are removed. The water surface's existing `sun_direction` input follows the same real light as a compatibility wire; surface reflection, shaft geometry, caustic tuning and full underwater daylight coupling remain P3 work. Sky, Environment and material objects are not recreated as time advances.

## Executed T2 checks

`./tools/dev build`, `./tools/dev import`, `./tools/dev inspect-solar-lighting`, `./tools/dev inspect-pond-atmosphere`, `./tools/dev inspect-pond-camera-immersion`, `./tools/dev inspect-depth`, `./tools/dev inspect-atmosphere`, and `./tools/dev inspect-environment-time` passed on Godot 4.7.2 .NET / .NET SDK 10.0.403. A separate Release C# build passed with zero warnings and errors. The final Debug build also passed with zero warnings and errors. The focused solar check sampled 1,441 minute positions per habitat plus fixed noon, dawn, morning, afternoon, sunset, twilight and night states; it checked finite bounded values, neighboring steps, and both sides of midnight/dawn/sunset. It read the real `Sky`, shader uniforms, `Environment`, directional light and surface sun-direction input, and asserted stable resource identities and unchanged fish position/velocity. Observed noon/night direct-light energy was `1.120/0.000` in the Ocean fixture and `1.000/0.000` in Pond air; ambient energy was `0.385/0.119` and `0.750/0.152` respectively. Pond→Ocean→Pond profile isolation, camera pause/reentry, depth and equal-movement atmosphere checks passed.

The first focused build failed because the new check used Color methods absent from Godot C#; it was corrected to inspect components. A first Ocean atmosphere check exposed stale fixture assumptions: it captured visibility before the camera settled, assumed a 100 m caustic decal in the expanded 386 m world, and expected the deep core to lie outside the newer tall decal. The check now samples the settled camera, scales to the actual decal and relies on the existing depth fade/fog checks for deep attenuation. An attempted direct GDScript call to a C# refresh method was removed after its parse/runtime failure. All revised focused paths passed; these intermediate failures are not counted as successful runs.

Headless checks establish state and shader/runtime integrity, not visual beauty. The night sky, horizon, shadows, reflective water and underwater readability still need P4 native judgment after P3 couples water and underwater effects to the same solar state.
