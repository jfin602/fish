Its underside highlight and illumination also respond to time.

Underwater sky brightness, ambient energy and direct-light color follow solar daylight through profile-specific floors. One shaft mesh steers its top vertices with a bounded sun-direction offset, and its brightness fades with direct light. One existing caustic decal varies emission and warm tint; no extra light, pass or per-frame resource creation was added. The pause-aware clock and presentation node both inherit gameplay pause, so the wave, shaft and decal animation time and their solar inputs freeze together.
