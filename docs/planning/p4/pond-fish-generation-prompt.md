**Use:** Standalone implementation prompt for the first Pond asset milestone. This is an authored prompt, not evidence of generated assets or a phase-runner stack. The owner has explicitly put fish first; isolated asset creation may proceed while Ocean world review continues. Do not integrate Pond into normal play or change the root version as part of this asset task.

## Prompt

Create the first original **Pond Fish Asset Factory pack** for Fish: **bluegill, largemouth bass, channel catfish, common carp and northern pike**, with **Egg, Larva, Juvenile, Subadult and Adult** for each species. Deliver **25 distinct, rigged stage assets**, editable Blender sources, reproducible construction code where useful, individual GLBs, manifests, validation and an isolated native Godot Forward+ review scene. Complete the full five-species pack; do not substitute concept art, flat sprites, unrigged meshes, scaled adult duplicates, or one generic fish in five colors.

### Modeling and life-stage progression

- Build each species as a recognizable family with stage-specific head/body proportions, eye size, fin development, tail shape, markings and color progression. Preserve a continuous identity from egg through adult. Document intended real-world bounds and verify imported bounds; do not use staging scale in final GLBs to conceal incorrect dimensions.
- Bluegill: deep compressed sunfish profile and its own fin/marking language. Largemouth bass: elongated bass profile and broad mouth. Channel catfish: bottom-oriented body, broad head and visible barbels. Common carp: heavy body, distinct mouth and short barbels. Northern pike: long narrow predatory profile and rearward fin emphasis. Keep silhouettes readable from side, top and three-quarter angles; do not force all species through one body loft.
- Make Egg, Larva, Juvenile, Subadult and Adult for **each** species. Early stages may be necessarily tiny, but their construction should be biologically plausible and visually inspectable in a zoomed review mode. Do not invent mature adult markings on every larva. Keep stage proportions and motion species-specific rather than uniform scale multipliers.
- Follow FAF modeling axes: Blender fish forward `-Y`, dorsal `+Z`, lateral `X`, origin at the main body pivot. Apply export transforms without breaking bind poses. Use intentional faceted surfaces, clean fin roots, simple materials and the existing starting budgets as guides; record measured exported counts rather than asserting a budget pass from an estimate.

### Rigs and required animation

- **Every one of the 25 stage exports must contain a real skin/armature and stage-appropriate deformation.** This is an explicit Pond-pack exception to the prior mesh-only FAF eggs. Egg rigs may be minimal but must deform a visible embryo or egg structure; they must not be a dummy root attached to a rigid mesh.
- For each Egg, export `Idle` as a subtle looping embryo/egg motion and `Hatch` as a one-shot stage-specific action. Eggs have **no fake swimming Actions**. Check that Hatch is genuinely animated and does not translate the gameplay root.
- For every Larva, Juvenile, Subadult and Adult, export distinct, nonempty, cleanly looping `Idle`, `Glide`, `SwimSlow`, `Swim` and `SwimFast` Actions, plus `TurnLeft` and `TurnRight` Actions with actual lateral deformation. Tune frequency, amplitude, fin motion and effort by species and life stage. Larvae should not swim as shrunken adults; catfish, carp, bass, bluegill and pike should not share identical wave profiles. No Action may move the game root through the world.
- Use the FAF `root → spine_* → tail_* → tail_fin` roles and species-appropriate fin/jaw/barbel controls. Keep heads/eyes stable and test both turn extremes, strongest swim, fin roots, barbels and tail junctions for clipping or collapse. All swimming stages are bony fish: declare `operculate_bony`, include exported `gill_L` and `gill_R` deform bones with localized bilateral weights, and validate neutral/subtle/strong breathing poses. Keep breathing bones neutral in swim Actions; Godot owns later breathing timing. Record any stage-specific anatomical exception explicitly rather than marking an unverified rig complete.
- Follow the existing FAF naming rule exactly: stage-suffixed non-adult Action names; canonical Adult names. Configure Godot import looping for loops only; `Hatch` remains one-shot. Verify actual Action contents after GLB import, not merely Action names in Blender.
