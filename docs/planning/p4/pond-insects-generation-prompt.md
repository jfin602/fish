# Pond FAF shallow-water insect generation prompt

**Use:** Standalone FAF asset-production prompt for the missing Pond insect pack. It creates four original ambient assets and an isolated native proof. P4 P8 later integrates existing assets; this prompt must not modify the active P4 runner, generated Pond world or normal play. Work in an isolated checkout while P4 integration is active and coordinate any shared ID/helper changes before merging.

## Prompt

Create the first original **Pond FAF shallow-water insect pack** for Fish: **one submerged aquatic larva family with two readable A/B variants, and one surface or swimming water-bug family with two readable A/B variants**. Deliver **four distinct Pond-only 3D assets**, editable Blender sources, individual GLBs with simple genuine animation, a manifest, focused validation, and an isolated Godot Forward+ proof. Use `/home/jfin/best-pond-faf.png` for the faceted olive-water style and shallow-bank composition. These insects are **ambient presentation only**. Do not add feeding, edible/prey values, attacks, damage, AI hunting, growth or ecology mechanics.

### Preflight and family choice

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `BOOT.md`, `docs/workflow.md`, `docs/planning/p4/pond-faf-assets.md`, `docs/tasks/p4/pond-faf-integration.md`, and the focused FAF art-style, asset-ID, modeling, animation, budget, Blender–Godot handoff and visual-review contracts. Inspect the current Pond fish, creature and foliage manifests/proof patterns, the actual Godot version, Git HEAD and working-tree changes. Preserve existing Pond/Ocean assets and ongoing P4 world integration.
2. Choose and record **one documented submerged larval insect group** and **one documented surface-dwelling or swimming water-bug group** before modeling. Both variants in each family should share recognizable anatomy; A/B should differ in proportion, leg/appendage form, size or markings rather than only hue. Use reliable morphology/motion references and make original geometry/materials. Do not copy third-party models or textures or commit the owner reference image without a provenance decision.
3. Reserve stable, nonoverlapping Pond FAF IDs after checking the live registry. Add `INSECT` to the FAF ID/naming helpers deliberately if that category is absent; use family IDs with A/B variant suffixes. Put source, GLBs, manifest, checker and proof in Pond-specific paths. Record `faf_habitat=pond`, exact group/type, `submerged` or `surface/swimming` placement, variant, dimensions and ambient-only role in both manifest and exported metadata. Coordinate shared registry edits with concurrent asset work rather than overwriting another branch.

### Model and animate four small assets

- **Submerged larva A/B:** Give both a readable head/body/abdomen and characteristic legs or aquatic appendages for the selected larval group. Keep the silhouette distinguishable from a tiny fish or a generic pebble. Variant B should materially alter body proportions or appendage spread while remaining the same family. Place them on/near shallow sediment, submerged wood or aquatic plants according to the chosen group.
- **Water-bug A/B:** Give both the selected group's characteristic body and leg arrangement. If surface-dwelling, author a believable waterline/contact stance with legs visible against the surface; if swimming, author a readable paddle/leg silhouette just below the surface. Variant B should differ in body or appendage shape and scale, not merely color. Do not mix unrelated insect families into one A/B pair.
- Use intentional low-poly facets, one or a few compact opaque materials, no photographic texture detail and minimal or no transparency. Spend geometry on the silhouette that survives at intended shallow-water viewing distance. Tiny assets may use modest exaggeration for readability, but record actual metre dimensions and do not silently enlarge the live world instance to fake a pass.
- Each variant needs a lightweight **real deforming rig or equivalent exported mesh animation** and at least one named, nonempty, seamless movement loop appropriate to its anatomy: larval leg/abdomen motion or short crawl/swim; surface skating or paddled swimming for water bugs. A restrained Idle loop may be included when it adds visible value. No loop may translate the gameplay root through the world. Godot later owns placement, heading and any slow ambient drift. Avoid elaborate combat or feeding Actions.

### Export, proof and validation

1. Save editable first-party `.blend` source and reproducible generator/recipe code where used. Export one clean GLB per variant with only intended mesh, material, rig/animation and metadata. Add a four-entry manifest with FAF ID, family, variant, placement zone, source/output path, exact dimensions, exported triangles/materials, rig/Action names and loop type, seed/generator version, provenance, and independent technical/style review states. Do not borrow an existing fish or creature ID.
2. Build an **isolated** Godot Forward+ proof with a shallow sloped mud bank, visible waterline, lily/reed/wood context and an open fish lane. Show both larva variants submerged and both water-bug variants in their selected surface/swimming zone. Provide close inspection and actual fish-camera-distance views from above, at the waterline and underwater. Review silhouettes against silt, algae, wood shadow and reflected/light surface areas; use movement, spacing and contrast to help readability without making every insect oversized or emissive.
3. Run focused checks for **exactly four GLBs** and two A/B families, unique Pond metadata/IDs, correct scale/orientation/material import, real animated deformation, loop seams, no animated game-root translation and placement at the documented water depth. Use representative sparse and denser clusters to record draw/triangle cost and whether repeated instances remain legible without clutter. Tiny insects normally need no collision. Do not infer feeding or gameplay readiness from their presence.
4. Capture each variant from a clear close angle and a realistic shallow-water game view, plus mixed-density and above/below-waterline evidence. Record Blender/Godot versions, source revision, actual checks, defects fixed and unobserved limits. Technical import success and native visual readability are separate findings; owner style acceptance remains a separate decision.

### Done condition

The pack is technically complete when the **two submerged larva variants and two surface/swimming water-bug variants** each have editable source, imported GLB, Pond-only identity, a genuine looping movement animation and matching manifest record. Native proof must show family-level silhouette differences, readable A/B variants and appropriate shallow-water placement at representative density. Report passed, failed and unobserved criteria honestly. Do not mark P4 G4.3 or P8 complete from the isolated pack; those require later intake and in-world/native evidence.
