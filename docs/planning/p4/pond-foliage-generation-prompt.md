# Pond FAF foliage generation prompt

**Use:** Standalone implementation prompt for the Pond's first foliage pack. It can be worked in an isolated checkout alongside the fish and creature packs if FAF IDs and shared-file ownership are coordinated first. This prompt creates assets and an isolated proof, not a Pond world, gameplay ecology or P4 Gate result.

## Prompt

Create an original **Pond Fish Asset Factory foliage pack** for Fish, in the low-poly style of the owner-supplied `/home/jfin/best-pond-faf.png`. Deliver these base families: **three lily-pad silhouettes with stems; two tall-grass clumps; two cattail clumps; two reed clumps; two submerged slime/algae shapes; and two floating muck/duckweed patches**. That is **13 distinct foliage variants plus the reusable lily-stem components needed to connect pads to the pond bottom**. Make editable Blender sources, clean individual GLBs, a deterministic recipe where useful, a manifest, focused import checks and an isolated native Godot Forward+ proof scene. Do not substitute flat concept art, photographic foliage cards or one mesh recolored into every family.

### Preflight and scope

1. Read `AGENTS.md`, `BOOT.md`, `docs/workflow.md`, `docs/planning/p4/pond-faf-assets.md`, and the focused FAF art-style, asset-ID, modeling, budget, Blender–Godot handoff and visual-review contracts. Inspect the existing `FAF-PLANT-001–003` family source, generator, manifest, UV height-mask rule and Godot proof/check fixture. Inspect Git HEAD and working-tree changes; preserve the Ocean plant library and in-progress P3 work.
2. Use the reference image for silhouette, layering and mood: large overlapping lily pads at the water ceiling, reeds and cattails forming vertical screens, uneven grasses at the banks, small green deposits on submerged wood/stone and scattered surface muck. Build original geometry and simple materials. Record source/provenance; do not commit unverified third-party meshes or textures.
3. Assign new stable FAF `PLANT` IDs after checking the live registry. Each A/B/C variant belongs to a clearly named family. Tag intended placement explicitly as `surface`, `shore`, `shallow_rooted` or `submerged`, with its anchor and waterline relationship; do not infer habitat or placement from display names. Use a Pond habitat tag. Keep all foliage files, recipe functions and proof scenes in a Pond-specific namespace. If another asset pack is running concurrently, reserve IDs and coordinate ownership of shared naming/registry files before edits; do not overwrite their changes.

### Build the families

- **Lily pads — three silhouettes plus stems:** Make three visibly different broad faceted pads with varied lobes, notches, edge lean and subtle color/size variation. Keep pad tops at the water surface. Provide reusable stem geometry or a reproducible stem builder with a clean attachment point under each pad and a bottom anchor; support more than one shallow depth without stretching a fixed stem unnaturally. Pads should overlap in curated clusters but leave bright water gaps and swimming sightlines.
- **Tall grasses — two clumps:** Make bank-rooted blade groups with different height/spread and a clear base pivot. Read as grasses from the player camera, not as the same reeds without seed heads. Keep roots fixed and tips available for gentle sway.
- **Cattails — two clumps:** Include long upright leaves and recognizable seed heads with differing stalk count/height. Root them in shallow mud; let the tops extend above the waterline when placed. Seed heads should remain legible without dense geometry.
- **Reeds — two clumps:** Make slimmer, more numerous vertical stalks than cattails, with uneven height/lean and sparse leaf detail. Use them to build layered screens without forming an impassable visual wall.
- **Submerged slime/algae — two shapes:** Make low, irregular attached growth for mud, rocks and logs. It should hug the host surface and read as organic buildup, not as a floating solid blob or a thick opaque carpet hiding collision edges.
- **Floating muck/duckweed — two patches:** Make irregular, lightweight surface clusters with broken edges and visible water openings. Place them at the waterline; allow gentle visual drift or phase variation without changing simulation state. Avoid large opaque sheets that cover the entire surface.

Use broad faceted shapes and a restrained olive, moss, yellow-green and brown palette that remains distinct from the existing reef greens. Prefer opaque or minimally transparent materials; use alpha only when it materially helps thin silhouettes, and inspect sorting/overdraw in Godot. Include UV or equivalent height/attachment data so rooted plants can sway from the tips while their bases stay fixed. Pad tops and floating patches may move subtly with the surface; submerged slime should mostly remain attached. Visual animation must not alter collision, fish movement or world simulation.

### Export, placement and proof

1. Save editable first-party `.blend` masters and source-controlled generator/recipe changes. Export one clean GLB per foliage variant, with separate reusable lily-stem assets if that is the selected construction. Keep export meshes/materials named, transforms applied, origins useful for placement and unused review objects excluded. Record exact dimensions, triangles, materials, seed/configuration, source and output paths, waterline/anchor rules, placement zone, habitat and style/technical state in a manifest.
2. Build an **isolated** Godot Forward+ proof with a sloped muddy bank, visible water surface, shallow submerged wood/stone and an open swim lane. Show each family alone and then in a mixed near/middle/distant arrangement. Include above-surface, waterline, underwater and actual follow-camera-distance views. Inspect pad/stem connection, rooted contact, waterline alignment, sway, transparency, overlapping silhouettes and apparent density against the reference image. Do not modify normal play, Ocean atmosphere, gameplay placement or the shared world compiler in this task.
3. Add focused checks for the exact family/variant inventory, unique Pond-tagged FAF IDs, GLB import, named material count, measured triangle count, dimensions, pivots, stem attachments, placement metadata, and height-mask/anchor data. Reproduce generator output for a fixed seed where generated. Confirm anchored bases remain still under sway and that surface pieces do not sink, hover or disappear from representative camera angles.
4. Capture family overviews, individual silhouettes and representative native Godot shallow-bank/density views. Record actual Blender/Godot versions, source revision, measured cost and observed defects/fixes. Native style review and representative density must be recorded separately from headless import success.

### Done condition

The pack is technically complete when all **13 required variants and lily-stem components** have editable sources, correct GLBs, noncolliding stable IDs, manifest records, verified placement anchors and focused import checks. Native review must show readable lily-pad surface clusters, bank grasses, distinct cattails/reeds, attached submerged algae and broken floating-muck patches, with gentle motion where appropriate and clear swimming routes. Report passed, failed and unobserved checks honestly; an isolated asset proof does not complete a P4 Gate.
