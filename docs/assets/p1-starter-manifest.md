Static Blender DNA inspection used Blender's official `tools/modules/blendfile.py` reader and `_blendfile_header.py` in `/tmp`, without opening Blender or running embedded scripts. These are **stored datablock facts**, not evaluated/exported geometry or animation quality:

- **Sea life:** 47 objects, 46 meshes, 11 materials, 18 image datablocks, zero armatures and zero actions. Named objects include Blue Fish, Yellow Tang, coral balls/stalks/fans, sea ferns/grass, plankton and a camera/light scene. There are 16 packed non-render images; names include four Blue Fish maps, four Yellow Tang maps and eight `RockMossyFull005` / `RockSpottyMoss009` maps with stored Poliigon paths. The fish meshes have no rig or swim action. Coral meshes are potential individual dressing pieces, but neither the scene nor its packed third-party textures are cleared for public reuse. [Poliigon's current terms](https://help.poliigon.com/en/articles/8749749-asset-use-licensing) prohibit sharing or embedding their assets in distributable scene files; original texture provenance must be resolved or the maps excluded before using a mesh with independent materials.
- **Great White Shark:** Blender 2.79 scene with 13 objects, seven meshes, two armature datablocks and one `PoseLib` action. The shark body, face and teeth objects are parented to an armature. There are 13 materials; the creator describes eight shark materials built in Cycles with micro-displacement and no external textures. Stored images are render/viewer outputs, not usable fish texture maps. The one pose-library action has 143 single-key curves, not a demonstrated swimming cycle. Strip scene water, lights and camera; rebuild/bake materials and test deformation before any later predator use.
- **Rigged Archer Fish:** Blender 2.79 scene with 145 objects/141 meshes, dominated by rig widget geometry; one armature object and one fish mesh (`1,418` vertices, `1,466` stored polygons). One action `rigAction.001` has 39 F-curves and 7,975 stored keyframes, but it is not named or verified as a swim loop. Five images are packed despite stale Windows-style texture paths. Two embedded text blocks include `rig_ui.py.001`; this vendor script was read only as a datablock and must not become Fish gameplay. Strip widget/studio objects, assess deform bones and action, and export a clean rig if ever used for a suitable estuary biome.

The archer fish is not the initial controllable fish: [FishBase](https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Toxotes_jaculatrix) and the [Australian Museum](https://australian.museum/learn/animals/fishes/sevenspot-archerfish-toxotes-chatareus/) describe archerfish as freshwater/brackish estuary fish. The shark is retained for later predator evaluation. Sea life's Yellow Tang is ocean-appropriate but unrigged/unanimated. These earlier candidate limitations led to the owner's Quaternius pack choice above. Fish3 has a stored rig and swim action; transfer to Godot remains P3 work.
